# PROJECT

Develop a complete Multi-Agent AI Workflow Automation Platform.

The goal is to automate project management work inside software companies.

The system should observe communications from Slack, Gmail, GitHub, meetings, and Jira.

It should understand what happened, determine required actions, coordinate specialized AI agents, and automatically execute project management tasks.

Example:

"Finished Login API.
Waiting for frontend."

↓

Update sprint progress

↓

Update manager dashboard

↓

Everything happens automatically.
